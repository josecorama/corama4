Co Founder & CEO

Adrián Rodríguez

Intro: Adrian is dedicated to public service, leveraging data, technology, and social science to design effective policies.

Educational Background: He holds a master's degree in Public Policy from the Harris School of Public Policy at the University of Chicago, and a bachelor's degree in law from the Autonomous University of Baja California.

Professional Experience: With experience in government, nonprofits, and the private sector, Adrian served as a legal affairs coordinator for the Mexican government and worked at the Consulate of Mexico in Fresno and Chicago, focusing on immigration.

Current Role: Currently, Adrian is the Director of Contract Radar Maximizer at the Illinois Hispanic Chamber of Commerce, where he leads innovative projects to streamline the bidding process for small, minority-owned, and women-owned businesses.

Recognition: Adrian was recognized among the "40 under 40" Latinos by "Negocios Now" in 2024, highlighting his dedication to public service.

Personal Background: Originally from California, Adrian grew up on the Mexico-U.S. border, shaping his bicultural and binational identity. He lives with his wife, Pauleth Jauregui, and daughter, Isabella.
