The World Chambers Competition started in 2003 and is an integral part of the World Chambers Congress. Organized by the ICC World Chambers Federation, it is the only global award that recognizes the most innovative projects undertaken by chambers of commerce and industry. By highlighting the success of local initiatives, the Competition serves to inspire the creation of pioneering and beneficial chamber solutions for business communities worldwide.

The Best Chamber Model Innovation project category specifically recognizes chambers that have worked with the CMI framework. The CMI enables Chambers to become resilient and forward-looking by harnessing the power of data and designing services that meet customers' needs.

In June 2023, Contract Radar Maximizer was honored to be among the top four finalists in the Best Chamber Model Innovation project category at the prestigious World Chambers Competition, held during the World Chambers Congress in Geneva, Switzerland. This recognition was awarded to chambers that have successfully utilized the Chamber Model Innovation (CMI) framework to become resilient and forward-looking by harnessing the power of data and designing services that meet customer needs. Contract Radar Maximizer's innovative approach in leveraging AI to help small businesses navigate government contracts stood out, showcasing our commitment to empowering communities and fostering economic growth.
